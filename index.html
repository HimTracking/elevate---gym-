OUR STORY / THE START

FROM ONE
ATHLETE TO A
COMMUNITY.

Elevate Athletics Academy began in April 2023 with a simple ambition: help athletes improve, and build a stronger future for athletics. Founded by national-level athlete and track & field coach Dinesh Godara, the academy has grown through consistent training, athlete dedication and a community that believes in progress.

We work around performance—not shortcuts. Every session is a step toward better movement, better numbers and a stronger mindset.
